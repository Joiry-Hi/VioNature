# V2.75 Space-Travel PLUS - Scavenger UFO, Hyperspace, and the Snap

VioNature has always been a strange FPS laboratory: part arena shooter, part movement toy, part configurable pile of impossible weapons.

This update pushes that identity much further into space.

The new version is centered on the **Scavenger UFO**: a special encounter that can become a vehicle, open a cockpit interface, collect Essence, fire its own weapons, and finally jump into a cylindrical hyperspace route toward a new battlefield. Alongside that, the Infinity Gauntlet now has the super move it was always quietly asking for: **the Snap**.

## The Scavenger UFO

The Scavenger UFO is attracted by the aftershock of a super ball-lightning explosion.

Once triggered, it arrives above the battlefield as a wandering collector from space. It searches for loose Essence, descends over it, and pulls it upward with a pale blue tractor beam. If it gathers enough Essence, it escapes. If the player gets too close, or if there is no Essence left to collect, it fights back with small homing ball-lightning orbs.

It is not just another boss health bar, though. If you shoot it down before it escapes, the UFO falls to the ground and stays there.

Then you can walk up to it and press **F**.

## From Boss to Vehicle

After being defeated, the UFO becomes a pilotable vehicle.

Entering it switches the game into a UFO cockpit / fire-control view. The player weapon model is hidden, the normal FPS framing gives way to a colder sci-fi interface, and the UFO lifts off into a hover state. From there, it can be flown around the arena with a new control layer:

- **WASD** to move
- **Space / Ctrl** to adjust altitude
- **Shift** to boost movement
- **Mouse look** to aim
- **F** to leave the UFO and park it in the air

The UFO can be entered again later, so defeating it effectively adds a persistent vehicle to the current run.

## UFO Weapons

The piloted UFO currently has two weapon modes, switched with the mouse wheel.

**Small Ball Lightning** fires a compact homing explosive orb. It is based on the UFO boss attack, but player-owned, tuned for fighting enemies rather than punishing the pilot.

**Tractor Beam** pulls Essence and enemies along the beam. Essence collected this way goes into the UFO's own internal tank, while enemies are displaced rather than directly damaged. It is part weapon, part utility tool, and part ridiculous physics experiment.

The cockpit HUD shows the UFO weapon state, UFO Essence storage, hyperspace readiness, and the player's own Essence count.

## Essence Sharing

The UFO now has an independent Essence pool.

That pool matters because it fuels hyperspace jumps, but it is not isolated from the player. While piloting the UFO:

- Hold or tap **Q** to store player Essence into the UFO
- Hold or tap **E** to extract Essence back from the UFO

This makes Essence feel less like a single survival meter and more like a resource you can route between body, machine, and travel. You can keep extra Essence as personal protection, feed it into the UFO for future jumps, or pull it back before entering a dangerous new fight.

Player Essence and UFO Essence are both preserved through hyperspace arrival.

## Hyperspace Tunnel

The biggest new mode is UFO hyperspace travel.

While piloting the UFO, hold **H** to charge a jump. If the UFO has enough Essence, the screen compresses, the jump fires, and the game leaves the normal FPS arena for a third-person hyperspace tunnel.

This tunnel is a cylindrical route through space. The UFO stays near the inner wall of the cylinder while obstacles and energy structures rush toward the player. Instead of moving endlessly through a giant level, the world streams past the UFO, giving the sense of high-speed travel without needing an actual infinite map.

Hyperspace controls are simple:

- **A / D** move around the cylinder wall
- **Space / Ctrl** adjust distance from the wall
- Avoid incoming obstacles when possible

Collisions cost UFO Essence and create feedback, but they do not immediately end the run. The focus for this first version is sensation: speed, tunnel motion, strange geometry, and the feeling that the arena shooter has briefly turned into a space-flight minigame.

When the hyperspace progress completes, the UFO arrives in a new battlefield.

## Arrival in a New World

Hyperspace arrival rebuilds the battlefield into a random new map and mode.

The player remains inside the UFO, with UFO Essence, player Essence, collected count, and selected UFO weapon preserved. In practice, this means the UFO can now carry a run from one arena into another. It is a first step toward using the UFO as connective tissue between VioNature's different maps, modes, and future space-travel systems.

New arrivals may also slightly vary world and enemy parameters, so hyperspace does not simply feel like pressing reset. The destination is still an FPS arena, but it can arrive with a different shape, rhythm, and pressure.

## Infinity Gauntlet: The Snap

The Infinity Gauntlet now has a super weapon mode.

While the Gauntlet is equipped, hold **left mouse + right mouse** together to charge the Snap. Once fully charged, release both buttons to spend Essence and roll a death check for every regular enemy on the field.

Each enemy is judged independently. Some vanish. Some survive.

The Essence cost, charge time, and kill probability are all configurable, so the Snap can be tuned anywhere from desperate gamble to world-ending button. It also has its own sound effect, because a cosmic coin flip should not be quiet.

## Audio and Presentation

This update also expands the game's audio identity:

- UFO encounter music
- Dedicated hyperspace music
- Hyperspace charge sound
- UFO orb ambience
- UFO weapon and cockpit feedback
- Snap sound effect

The goal is to make the UFO chain feel like a full event: something that starts as a boss encounter, becomes a machine you can claim, and then opens a route out of the current arena.

## Quick Control Reference

**Enter / leave UFO:** F near a landed or parked UFO  
**UFO weapon switch:** Mouse wheel  
**Fire UFO weapon:** Left mouse  
**Store Essence into UFO:** Q  
**Extract Essence from UFO:** E  
**Charge hyperspace jump:** Hold H  
**Gauntlet Snap:** Equip Infinity Gauntlet, hold LMB + RMB, then release after charging

## Notes

This is still an experimental system.

The UFO is not just a boss, and hyperspace is not just a transition screen. The long-term idea is that the UFO can become a bridge between worlds, modes, and future space-flight content. This release builds the first complete loop: summon it, fight it, steal it, fly it, fuel it, jump with it, and arrive somewhere else still holding the controls.

That is very much the kind of mutation VioNature was built for.

Thanks for playing, testing, and following the project as it keeps turning into stranger machinery.
